Privacy Policy for Carry Studio

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Carry Studio is an internal creator workflow tool. We only process:

  • User authorization credentials (OAuth 2.0 access tokens) required to interact with the TikTok API.
  • Carousel media files (1080x1350 JPEG images) and localized text captions for posting purposes.

2. How Information is Used

  • Authenticate and communicate securely with the TikTok Developer Platform on your behalf.
  • Upload photo media packages and schedule carousels to the designated TikTok account (@carry.vietnam.tw).

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All sensitive tokens and credentials are encrypted or stored locally within the user's controlled private environment.
  • To let TikTok retrieve a carousel, its images are placed temporarily in this site's media folder and are deleted once the creator has published the post in the TikTok app (or by the operator if the draft is discarded).
  • We do not collect, store or process any data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.
  • We never sell, rent, or transmit personal credentials to unauthorized third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Carry Studio connects to TikTok via the official TikTok Developer Platform. For information on how TikTok processes your data, please refer to TikTok's official Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or inquiries, please contact the repository administrator.
